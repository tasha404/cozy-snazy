About Us

A family kitchen rooted in the belief that the best food is made slowly, with real ingredients, and shared with people you love.

our story

Three generations
in one kitchen

Cozy Coffee started in 1978 when Marta Kowalski opened a small kitchen out of her home in Atlanta, cooking the food she'd grown up eating in her grandmother's house in Kraków. Word spread fast. By 1982 she had a dining room. By 1990, a full restaurant.

Today, Marta's daughter Rosa runs the kitchen alongside her own children. The recipes haven't changed. The ingredients haven't changed. Even some of the pots are the same ones Marta bought in 1978.

We don't believe in shortcuts. Everything on the menu is made from scratch, every morning, by the same family that's been doing this for nearly fifty years.

what we stand for

Our values

🌿

Scratch-made, always

No pre-packaged sauces, no frozen ingredients. Every dish starts raw, prepared the same morning it's served.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Family first

We're not a chain or a brand. We're a family who loves to cook and loves feeding people. That's the beginning and end of it.

🌍

Seasonal & local

Our menu changes when the seasons do. We buy from local farmers and markets whenever we can, and always will.

🤝

Open to everyone

Good food shouldn't be exclusive. Prices honest, portions generous, doors open to whoever walks through them.
